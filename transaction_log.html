WHAT Is ComplianceTest?

ComplianceTest is an online self-service tool for organisations to test their products or services for compliance to data exchange standards defined by standards issuing authorities.

Issuers

Define compliance test suites for your standards - and host actual test interfaces, avoiding the cost of building and maintaining your own.

Implementers

Quickly find relevant test suites, test your products and services, and register your compliance claims. Discuss issues or problem with others in your community. The quickest and simplest way to achieve confidence in the compliance of your products & services.

More Information    »

Community

Leverage the knowledge of a community of interest to get help with your implementation.

Support

Engage the help of our experts when you need it.

Confidence

Finding and resolving problems early is MUCH cheaper than dealing with failed transactions or upset customers later.

Visibility

One place to find test suites and compliant products & services.

Cost

Don't want to build and manage your own test harnesses?

More Information    »

Test Suites

Standards issuers can publish test suites and implementers can search and view the test suites - for free.


Collaboration

Join any standards community (aafter approval by the issuer) and leverage community forums - for free.

Product Repository

Register your products and/or services and declare compliance with standards. Claims can be un-verified (becuase you've done testing elsewhere and just want to list your product) or can be verified (requires you to complete a test suite on compliancetest.net). Making a claim is free. Running a test may attract a fee.

Test Harness

ComplianceTest offers a dedicated test harness for each version of each standard. Testers can connect to the test harness and work through the test cases until completed - and keep an audit log as evidence of successful completion. Every test suite is available on a monthly subscription basis. In some cases, the monthly subscription is free (becuase the issuer has paid for the test harness implementation).
